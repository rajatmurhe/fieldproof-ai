==============================

Project:  FieldProof AI
Repo:     https://github.com/rajatmurhe/fieldproof-ai
Track:    Work & automation
Built by: Rajat Murhe (github.com/rajatmurhe)


What it is
----------
FieldProof AI automates quality checks for field service work. Field service
companies collect many job videos from technicians, but supervisors cannot
watch them all, so missed steps slip through.

FieldProof AI checks each technician job video against a configurable
checklist and returns a PASS, FAIL or NOT_VISIBLE result for every step,
with a confidence score, a short explanation and the timestamp where the
evidence appears. Supervisors review only what needs attention, approve or
correct it, and every decision is saved in an audit trail.


How it works
------------
* Multi tenant: each organisation's data is kept separate (Clerk).
* Videos are processed in a PostgreSQL job queue (SELECT FOR UPDATE SKIP
  LOCKED) with retries and stale job recovery, so processing keeps running
  without the frontend open.
* Human in the loop review with a full audit trail of each job.

Stack: Python, FastAPI, Next.js, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, SQLAlchemy, Clerk.


How it works with BharatCode
----------------------------
Integration in progress. The BharatCode model API is being added to:
* turn a supervisor's plain English instructions into a structured checklist
* write the final job report and reviewer summary from the video findings

Video understanding currently runs on Gemini. This section will be updated
with the exact models and setup steps once the BharatCode integration ships.


Notes
-----
No secrets, tokens or private data are stored in this repo. API keys are
read from environment variables.
